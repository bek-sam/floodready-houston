## Part A: next ideas (each <= 1.5 h, free verified data, no runtime Gemini)

Ranked by (demo value x safety) / effort, after the audit items above.

1. **Pet and livestock safety card** (0.75 h, client static). Great physical-world demo and partner story (city/HOA). Skip the iframe widget (2 h, frame headers, little safety value).
