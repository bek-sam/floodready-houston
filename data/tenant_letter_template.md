## Template A: request flood disclosure notices (before or at lease signing)

{date}

To: {landlord_name}
Re: {address}

Dear {landlord_name},

I am {name}, applying to rent or renting the dwelling at {address}. Texas Property Code 92.0135 asks a landlord to give a separate written notice, at or before lease signing, saying whether the landlord is aware the dwelling is in a 100-year floodplain, and whether it has flooded at least once in the last five years.

Please send me both written notices. I would like them before I sign or, if I have already signed, as soon as possible.

Thank you,
{name}
{phone}

## Template B: end the lease after flood damage to belongings

{date}

To: {landlord_name}
Re: {address}

Dear {landlord_name},

I am {name}, a tenant at {address}. On {flood_date} flooding damaged my personal property. The repair or replacement cost is 50% or more of its market value on that date. I did not receive the flood notices described in Texas Property Code 92.0135 [fill in only if true].

Under that section, I am ending my lease by this written notice, which I am sending within 30 days of the damage. I will give up possession on {move_out_date}. Please refund any rent paid in advance for the period after that date within 30 days, as the section provides.

Sincerely,
{name}
{phone}

Tips: fill in only what is true, keep a copy, send by a method that gives proof of delivery, and keep dated photos and a list of damaged items.
