# Driving in floods: underpasses and "Turn Around, Don't Drown"
source: https://digitalcommons.library.tmc.edu/dissertations/AAI1470211
source: https://repository.lsu.edu/gradschool_theses/5466
source: https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/18/1073/2018/nhess-18-1073-2018.pdf
source: https://cs.observer-reporter.com/news/2016/apr/21/driver-deaths-spur-investigation/
source: https://www.click2houston.com/news/local/2026/06/16/this-is-not-over-harris-county-braces-for-more-flooding/

- 77% of Texas flood fatalities (1959–2008) involved driving into flood water. (TMC dissertation)
- Harris County has the most vehicle flood deaths in Texas, far more than Dallas, which had "almost seven times less" despite flooding more often. (LSU thesis)
- The "Turn Around Don't Drown" slogan shows "no convincing evidence" of reducing deaths, so timely, personal warnings matter. (LSU thesis)
- 81% of 70 Harvey deaths were drownings, "particularly in and around vehicles"; a family of 6 died when their van "was swept off a flooded road." (NHESS 2018)
- Three drivers died at the same underpass in April 2016, after earlier drownings there. Only 19 of 27 planned sensor and flashing-light sites were installed. Drivers went around a gate and crashed into it. (Observer-Reporter)
- In June 2026, "vehicles became stuck under bridges" in downtown Houston. (KPRC)
- Harvey study authors say high-hazard crossings "have not been adequately delineated or communicated to the public." (NHESS 2018)

What to do: never drive through water over a road or into an underpass; take another route before you leave. Use the FloodReady route check to see listed flood-prone crossings. In an emergency, call 911.
