# Tenant flood disclosure (Texas Property Code 92.0135)
source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.92.htm
source: https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_92.0135

Not legal advice. This is a plain summary for information only. Read the statute, and call a tenant legal-aid group if you need advice. Verification note: the capitol.texas.gov page did not render for our fetch, so this summary was checked against texas.public.law. Confirm on the official site.

- Applies to residential leases. Added by HB 531 (87th Legislature), effective January 1, 2022.
- Floodplain notice: the landlord must give the tenant a written notice saying whether the landlord is or is not aware that the dwelling is in a 100-year floodplain. If neither box is checked, assume it is in the floodplain. A home outside the floodplain can still flood, and most renters insurance does not cover flood.
- Exception: the landlord need not disclose if the dwelling has been elevated above floodplain levels under federal rules.
- Five-year flood history: if the landlord knows flooding damaged any part of the dwelling at least once in the 5 years before the lease starts, the landlord must give a notice saying so.
- Timing: both notices must be in a separate written document, given at or before lease signing.
- Remedy: if the landlord broke this rule and flooding damages your personal property worth 50% or more of its market value, you may end the lease by written notice no later than 30 days after the damage. It takes effect when you give up possession.
- Refund: the landlord must refund prepaid rent for the time after that date within 30 days.
- You still owe any rent or sums due before you ended the lease.
- This covers flood notices. It does not cover displacement from a government buyout; for buyouts, call Harris County Housing and Community Development.

What to do: ask for the notices before you sign, keep copies, take dated photos of your belongings, and send any termination notice in writing and keep proof of delivery.
