# Vulnerable households: older adults, disabled residents, medical needs
source: https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/18/1073/2018/nhess-18-1073-2018.pdf
source: https://www.readyharris.org/Newsroom/News-Releases/Ready-Harris-All-News-Releases/harris-county-launches-accessible-alert-system

- 56% of Harvey victims were over 50 and 29% were over 65; 70% were male. (NHESS 2018)
- Harris County has the "2nd largest population of older adults with disabilities" in the US. (ReadyHarris)
- 4 Harvey deaths came from "lack of medical treatment" (dialysis, asthma, heart care). (NHESS 2018)
- Texas Medical Center "essentially shut down" in Allison, and a hospital was evacuated during Imelda; bus and rail shut down and IAH closed. (HCFCD timeline; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Imelda_(2019))
- Electrocution caused 4 Harvey deaths. (NHESS 2018)

What to do: households with medical needs should plan before a storm: know backup options for dialysis or oxygen, keep medications and a power plan, and arrange a check-in with someone who can help. In an emergency, call 911.
