# Power outages, generators and carbon monoxide
source: https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html

- Carbon monoxide (CO) is a colorless, odorless gas that generators, grills, camp stoves and cars produce. It can kill. (CDC)
- Run a generator outdoors only, never indoors, in a garage or in a carport, and keep it more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents. (CDC)
- Install a battery-powered or battery-backup CO alarm and test it. (CDC)
- CO poisoning symptoms: headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain and confusion. (CDC)
- If you have these symptoms, get outside to fresh air right away and call 911. Do not go back inside until emergency responders say it is safe. (CDC)

What to do: after a storm, place any generator outdoors and well away from the house before starting it. This is general safety information, not official guidance. Call 911 in an emergency.
