# Mobile and manufactured homes in floods and storms
source: https://www.fema.gov/print/pdf/node/695591
source: https://www.fema.gov/zh-hans/print/pdf/node/691040

- FEMA advises occupants of manufactured housing not to take refuge in the unit during high winds or floods. (FEMA)
- Evacuate immediately when officials say to. (FEMA)
- In a flash flood warning, move to higher ground. (FEMA)

What to do: decide in advance where you will go (a friend's or relative's home or a shelter) and how you will get there, and leave early. This is not official guidance for your specific home. Call 911 in an emergency.
