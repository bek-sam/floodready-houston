# Mold cleanup after a flood
source: https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-cleanup-your-home

- For moldy areas under about 10 square feet (roughly 3 ft by 3 ft), you can usually clean it yourself. (EPA, https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-cleanup-your-home)
- Scrub hard surfaces with detergent and water, then dry them completely. (EPA, same page)
- Porous items such as ceiling tiles and carpet "may have to be thrown away". (EPA, same page)
- For larger areas or extensive damage, see EPA's larger-area guidance or consult a professional. (EPA, same page)

What to do: follow the steps above and see a doctor for lasting symptoms. Call 911 for emergencies. This card is general information, not official guidance.
