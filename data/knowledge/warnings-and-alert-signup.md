# Flood warnings and alert sign-up
source: https://harriscountyfws.org/Help
source: https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api
source: https://www.readyharris.org/Newsroom/News-Releases/Ready-Harris-All-News-Releases/harris-county-launches-accessible-alert-system
source: https://traffic.houstontranstar.org/api/api_doc.aspx

- The Harris County Flood Warning System gage map "is not intended as a notification system," "does not show all flooding," misses street ponding and ungaged channels, "does not include water depth," and lags up to 15 minutes. (harriscountyfws.org Help)
- Signing up for FWS alerts takes effort: "You will not receive ALERTS! until the account is validated." (FWS Help)
- NWS alerts are by area, county or zone; there is no road-level endpoint. (weather.gov API docs)
- ReadyHarris sends alerts and family plans in 5 languages, but accessible alerts (ASL, voice, Braille) exist only in English and Spanish. Harris County has the 5th largest population of people with disabilities in the US. (ReadyHarris)
- TranStar's live roadway flood feed is not public: "to inquire about gaining access to the live data feeds, please contact Houston TranStar." (TranStar)
- Flash floods leave little time; rainfall of 8-10 inches in three hours has occurred. (Rice; HCFCD timeline https://www.hcfcd.org/portals/62/about/flooding-history/historytimeline-24x36-1.pdf)

What to do: sign up for ReadyHarris alerts and the county FWS (and validate the account) before a storm. Turn on phone emergency alerts.
