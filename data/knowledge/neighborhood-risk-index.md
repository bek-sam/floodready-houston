# FEMA National Risk Index (neighborhood context)
source: https://hazards.fema.gov/nri/
source: https://services.arcgis.com/XG15cJAlne2vxtgt/arcgis/rest/services/National_Risk_Index_Census_Tracts/FeatureServer/0/query

- The National Risk Index (NRI) is a FEMA dataset that rates natural-hazard risk for every US county and census tract, including inland flooding (riverine). (FEMA NRI)
- Ratings use a five-step scale: Very Low, Relatively Low, Relatively Moderate, Relatively High, Very High. They compare a tract with other tracts nationally. (FEMA NRI)
- Community resilience and social vulnerability are rated separately. They describe a neighborhood's ability to prepare for and recover from hazards and its demographic factors. They are not a judgment about any household. (FEMA NRI)
- Caveat: this is a statistical index for the census tract, not your house. Use your FEMA flood zone, elevation and flood history for property-level risk.

What to do: use the index as context only. Call 911 in an emergency.
