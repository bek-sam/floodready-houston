# NFIP flood insurance claim: proof of loss
source: https://www.fema.gov/fact-sheet/submit-flood-insurance-proof-loss-form-within-60-days-loss

- Submit your Proof of Loss form within 60 days after the date of loss, even if you disagree with the adjuster's estimate. (FEMA, https://www.fema.gov/fact-sheet/submit-flood-insurance-proof-loss-form-within-60-days-loss)
- Include your signature, a building damage estimate and a personal-property inventory. A supplemental claim can be filed later. (FEMA, same page)
- Deadlines can be extended for specific events through FEMA bulletins, so ask your insurer whether an extension applies.

What to do: write down the date of loss, count 60 days from it, and ask your insurer or agent about any extension. This is separate from the 30-day waiting period for new policies. Call 911 for emergencies. This card is general information, not official guidance.
