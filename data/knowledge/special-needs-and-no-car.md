# Special needs, dialysis and no car: STEAR, 211 and 311
source: https://tdem.texas.gov/stear

## STEAR (State of Texas Emergency Assistance Registry)
- STEAR is a free, voluntary registry that gives local emergency planners and responders information about residents who may need extra help. (TDEM)
- It is meant for people with disabilities, people who are medically fragile, and people with access or functional needs, such as limited mobility, communication barriers, or needs for transportation or personal care assistance. (TDEM)
- The form asks for your name, address, phone and primary language, plus emergency contacts, caregivers, pets, evacuation transportation needs and medical needs. (TDEM)
- Registration does not guarantee help, and "available services will vary by community." (TDEM)

What to do: register before a storm, online at stear.texas.gov, or by dialing 2-1-1 (video relay users: 877-541-7905). Paper forms, including Spanish, are on https://tdem.texas.gov/stear. Tell your caregivers you registered. In an emergency, call 911.

## 211 and 311
- Dial 2-1-1 or (877) 541-7905. 211 Texas lists evacuation information, evacuation transportation, shelter assistance, extreme weather shelters and disaster recovery centers among its service categories. (211 Texas) The page does not say who qualifies, so ask when you call.
- Ready Harris links guidance on when to call 211, 311 and 911, and offers Accessible Alerts registration. (ReadyHarris)

What to do: call 211 early, before the storm, to ask about evacuation transportation and shelters. Call 311 for non-emergency city services. Call 911 for danger to life.

## Dialysis and medical needs
- If you can't get dialysis in an emergency, the CDC says to follow the 3-Day Emergency Diet, which "could save your life." (CDC)
- Keep phone numbers for your doctor, your regular dialysis center and another nearby dialysis center, a medication list, and at least a 3-day supply of each medicine. (CDC)
- Tell shelter staff about your medical needs. Make sure your dialysis center has your current phone and address. Register with your water and power companies in advance for priority restoration. (CDC)
- Help lines: National Kidney Foundation 1-855-653-2273; Kidney Community Emergency Response (KCER) 1-866-901-3773. (CDC)

What to do: ask your clinic now for its backup plan and the emergency diet, and review the diet with your dietitian. Pack medicines and phone numbers. If you use a home machine, plan for power loss. Call 911 in an emergency.
