# FEMA individual assistance and disaster declarations
source: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/program
source: https://www.fema.gov/api/open/v2/DisasterDeclarationsSummaries

- FEMA's Individuals and Households Program can give temporary housing and rental help, repair funds, and "other needs" help. You can apply online, by phone or in person, through DisasterAssistance.gov. (FEMA, https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/program)
- That FEMA page states no application deadline. Do not assume one; confirm the deadline at DisasterAssistance.gov.
- Aid depends on a federal declaration. As of 2026-10-11 the newest Harris County declaration in OpenFEMA is DR-4798 Hurricane Beryl (2024-07-09), then DR-4781 severe storms and flooding (2024-05-17). No federal declaration was found for the June 2026 flood; only a state declaration was reported. (OpenFEMA, https://www.fema.gov/api/open/v2/DisasterDeclarationsSummaries)

What to do: document damage with photos and receipts now. If a federal declaration is active, apply at DisasterAssistance.gov. If not, insurance and other steps still apply. Call 911 for emergencies. This card is general information, not official guidance.
