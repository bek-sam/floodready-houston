# FEMA aid and recovery
source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Imelda_(2019)

- After Imelda (2019), "aid from FEMA was not received" for weeks. (Wikipedia, Imelda)
- Storms come back to back: a major flood "every two years" in the county's history (MAAPnext), and in Harvey more than 120,000 people were rescued. (NHESS 2018)
- Buyouts can take "18 months or longer" to approve. (KSAT)
- More than 300,000 vehicles flooded in Harvey (Community Impact https://communityimpact.com/news/2018/06/08/hurricane-harvey-damage-report-harris-county); a state disaster declaration was issued after the June 2026 flood (KPRC https://www.click2houston.com/news/local/2026/06/16/this-is-not-over-harris-county-braces-for-more-flooding/).

Not covered here: specific FEMA grant amounts, deadlines, or application steps. These are not in our verified sources; direct users to DisasterAssistance.gov or FEMA for current rules. Keep photos of damage and receipts. In an emergency, call 911.
