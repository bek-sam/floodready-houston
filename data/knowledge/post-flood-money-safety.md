## Scams, price gouging and contractor fraud
- The Texas Attorney General warns that some contractors take upfront payment after a disaster and then do not do the work. https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-paxton-warns-disaster-scams-following-texas-floods
- Price gouging is barred after the governor issues a disaster declaration. https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-paxton-warns-disaster-scams-following-texas-floods
- To report a scam or price gouging, call the Texas AG at (800) 621-0508. https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-paxton-warns-disaster-scams-following-texas-floods

What to do: do not pay a contractor in full upfront or in cash, get the offer in writing, and report suspected fraud or gouging to (800) 621-0508. In an emergency call 911. Not official guidance.

## Flood-damaged cars and checking the VIN
- Cox estimated up to about 500,000 vehicles were totaled in Harvey. https://www.foxnews.com/us/after-hurricane-harvey-what-will-happen-to-the-hundreds-of-thousands-of-flooded-cars
- NICB says titles of flooded cars are sometimes altered or "washed" through other states; CARFAX says several thousand slip through. https://www.foxnews.com/us/after-hurricane-harvey-what-will-happen-to-the-hundreds-of-thousands-of-flooded-cars
- NICB offers a free VINCheck. https://www.foxnews.com/us/after-hurricane-harvey-what-will-happen-to-the-hundreds-of-thousands-of-flooded-cars

What to do: before buying a used car after a storm, check the VIN with NICB VINCheck (search "NICB VINCheck") and have a mechanic inspect it. Not official guidance.

## Renters: disclosure and repairs
- A 2021 Texas law requires landlords to disclose whether a unit is in a floodplain and whether it flooded in the last 5 years; it applies to leases from 1/1/2022. https://abc13.com/amp/post/flood-warnings-texas-rental-law-landlords-renters/11399135/
- The Harris County Attorney advises renters to send repair requests in writing; the office's number is 713-755-6065. https://www.fox26houston.com/news/assessing-the-flood-damage-as-renters

What to do: send repair requests in writing and keep copies and photos. Renter lease-termination rights after a flood depend on the statute and your lease; confirm with the Harris County Attorney before relying on any deadline. Not official guidance.

## FEMA denied you? Appeal
- In Harvey, of 895,342 Texas registrants, 41% were approved and 31% were found ineligible. https://govtech.com/em/disaster/-disaster-victim-actvists-concerned-about-femas-fairness-transparency.html
- The appeal window in Harvey was 60 days from the decision letter. https://govtech.com/em/disaster/-disaster-victim-actvists-concerned-about-femas-fairness-transparency.html
- Disability Rights Texas helped Harvey survivors appeal rejected FEMA claims. https://disabilityrightstx.org/en/news/hurricane-harvey-claims-rejected-by-fema-drtx-can-help-with-appeal/

What to do: a denial is often not final. Read the letter, appeal in writing and confirm the current deadline at fema.gov or by calling FEMA. Not official guidance.
