# Renters, disclosure and buyouts
source: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/harvey-reservoirs
source: https://planetizen.com/node/103514
source: https://www.ksat.com/news/texas/2024/05/20/the-government-wants-to-buy-their-flood-prone-homes-but-these-texans-arent-moving/
source: https://hcd.harriscountytx.gov/Disaster-Recovery/Buyout-Relocation-Programs/Involuntary-Buyout-Relocation-Program

- More than 475,000 Harris County residents live in at-risk multifamily housing (2019). (Planetizen)
- ProPublica reported in 2017 that neither the city nor the county required flood-risk disclosure; a renter said: "When I started to rent this house, nobody told me." Texas now requires landlords to give written flood notice (Property Code 92.0135, see tenant-flood-disclosure.md). (ProPublica)
- Harris County (excluding Houston) has 869 Severe Repetitive Loss properties with $400M in NFIP claims paid. (Harris County HCD)
- Buyouts are slow: "18 months or longer to approve a buyout application," and residents ask "Who do I call?" Offers may not cover a move. (KSAT)
- Renters are displaced by buyouts: for 114 demolitions, only 28 affordable units are to be replaced, in distant ZIP codes. (KSAT; HCD)

What to do: renters should ask the landlord about past flooding in writing, consider renters insurance with a separate flood policy, and know where to call. Buyout questions go to Harris County Housing and Community Development (see the civic page).

## If you rent and a buyout is planned (federal relocation rights)
source: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-A/part-24
source: https://hcd.harriscountytx.gov/Disaster-Recovery/Buyout-Relocation-Programs/

- If a buyout or acquisition uses federal money (for example FEMA or HUD funds), tenants who are displaced may be entitled to relocation help under the Uniform Relocation Act (49 CFR Part 24): advance written notice, advisory services and, if you qualify, moving and rent-replacement payments. (eCFR 49 CFR Part 24)
- This does not apply to purely local or voluntary programs without federal funds, and eligibility depends on the specific program. No amount is guaranteed.
- Harris County's Buyout & Relocation Program says owners inside buyout areas receive an official "General Information Notice". Ask the program in writing whether federal funds are used and whether tenants get the same notice. (Harris County HCD)
- Letter to send: ask (1) whether federal funds are involved, (2) for the written general information notice, (3) for relocation advisory services and an eligibility determination. Keep your lease and rent receipts.
- This is not legal advice and not official guidance. For legal help, contact a tenant legal aid office. In danger, call 911.
