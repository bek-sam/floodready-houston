# SBA disaster loans for homeowners and renters
source: https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/disaster-assistance/physical-damage-loans

- SBA low-interest disaster loans cover physical damage in declared disasters. Homeowners can borrow up to $500,000 to repair or replace the primary residence. (SBA, https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/disaster-assistance/physical-damage-loans)
- Renters and homeowners can borrow up to $100,000 for personal property such as clothing, furniture, cars and appliances. (SBA, same page)
- Insurance payouts may be deducted from the loan amount. (SBA, same page)
- SBA and FEMA declarations can differ, so check whether your area is declared.

What to do: if your area is declared, review the SBA page and apply. Renters can use this for belongings. Call 911 for emergencies. This card is general information, not official guidance.
