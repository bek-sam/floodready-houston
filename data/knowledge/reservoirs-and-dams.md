# Addicks and Barker reservoirs
source: https://www.texastribune.org/2017/09/28/could-houstons-dams-have-failed-during-harvey/
source: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/harvey-reservoirs
source: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/harvey

- Addicks and Barker reservoirs date from the 1940s. The Corps found a "high risk of failure due to seepage and piping"; more than $100M in repairs were needed. A 2010 Corps document using the phrase "risk of catastrophic failure" was not made public. (Texas Tribune)
- Harvey releases flooded homes downstream. 5,138 of about 14,000 homes inside the reservoirs flooded, and at least 4,000 of them were built after Allison. (ProPublica; Texas Tribune)
- As of 2017 ProPublica reporting, Harris County and Houston did not require flood-risk disclosure to buyers. Texas law has since added a landlord flood notice (Property Code 92.0135, since 2022) and a seller's disclosure of floodplain and reservoir status (SB 339, 2019). (ProPublica; https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/86R/analysis/pdf/SB00339S.pdf)
- Gauges on Langham Creek, Bear Creek and Buffalo Bayou at the reservoirs are in data/gauges.json.

What to do: if you live near Addicks or Barker, check whether your address is in the reservoir pool area and watch the reservoir gauges during heavy rain.
