# Addicks and Barker reservoirs
source: https://www.texastribune.org/2017/09/28/could-houstons-dams-have-failed-during-harvey/
source: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/harvey-reservoirs
source: https://projects.propublica.org/graphics/harvey

- Addicks and Barker reservoirs date from the 1940s and were rated "extremely high risk." The Corps found a "high risk of failure due to seepage and piping"; more than $100M in repairs were needed, and the 2010 risk document was never made public. (Texas Tribune)
- Harvey releases flooded homes downstream. 5,138 of about 14,000 homes inside the reservoirs flooded, and about 4,000 of them were built after Allison. (ProPublica; Texas Tribune)
- 167,000 acres were developed in the decade after Allison. (ProPublica)
- Neither the city nor the county requires disclosure to buyers; the only warning is a plat note. (ProPublica)
- Gauges on Langham Creek, Bear Creek and Buffalo Bayou at the reservoirs are in data/gauges.json.

What to do: if you live near Addicks or Barker, check whether your address is in the reservoir pool area and watch the reservoir gauges during heavy rain.
